# Toolkit artwork

Tool logos are sourced from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) under its MIT license, with additional branded icons from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) under CC0. Source license texts are included alongside these assets.

The original SVG artwork is scaled into a 64 px tile with a rounded background. Brand names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.
